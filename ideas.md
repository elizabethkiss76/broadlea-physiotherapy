<idea>
**Design Movement:** Scandinavian Clinical Minimalism
**Core Principles:**
- Radical whitespace as a signal of precision and care
- Monochromatic palette punctuated by a single warm accent
- Typography-first hierarchy; imagery is secondary
- Structured asymmetry: left-anchored text, right-side imagery

**Color Philosophy:** Near-white background (#F9FAFB) with deep forest teal (#0D5C63) as primary and warm sand (#E8DCC8) as accent. Communicates calm, trust, and clinical expertise without the sterility of pure blue.

**Layout Paradigm:** Offset grid — text blocks float left at 55% width while images bleed to the right edge. Section dividers are thin horizontal rules, not full-width color blocks.

**Signature Elements:**
- Thin-rule section separators with small circular ornaments
- Oversized section numerals (01, 02, 03) as decorative anchors
- Subtle grain texture overlay on hero section

**Interaction Philosophy:** Deliberate, unhurried. Hover states reveal information rather than transform elements. CTA buttons expand width on hover.

**Animation:** Staggered fade-up on scroll (60ms delay per item), hero text reveals character by character, smooth 200ms easing on all transitions.

**Typography System:** Playfair Display (headings, italic for emphasis) + DM Sans (body, 400/500). Large display sizes (72px hero), tight letter-spacing on headings.

**Signature Elements:**
- Bold diagonal section dividers using CSS clip-path
- Large typographic numbers as section anchors
- Geometric line illustrations as decorative elements

**Interaction Philosophy:** Confident and direct. Navigation highlights with an underline that slides in from left. Buttons have a sharp corner-to-corner fill animation on hover.

**Animation:** Clip-path reveal animations on section entry, counter animations for statistics, 150ms snappy transitions throughout.

**Typography System:** Space Grotesk (headings, bold geometric) + Inter (body, neutral clarity). Headings at 900 weight for maximum impact.
